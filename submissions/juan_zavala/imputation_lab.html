    Why every method guessed low

    Week 12 averaged 141.6 rushing yards, the 5th-highest of 18 weeks but inside the normal range (other weeks: 102.8 to 149.0, p = 0.31). It was a heavy rushing week by chance, so all three methods came in low on average.

    Data: the class's season_stats.csv (272 games, 16 missing rush_yds values, all in week 12) and actual.csv (the answer key). For weeks other than 12, the predictions come from the hidden-week test: each week was predicted from the other 16 known weeks.
